Current talk

Engineering pragmatic AI systems, local architectures, and automated operational pipelines

For engineers, technical directors and operations heads who need the practical realities rather than the marketing.

Most enterprise failures do not come from the models. They come from integration friction: connecting messy operational data safely into legacy databases, holding the line on privacy, and proving the numbers are right.

The session covers data ingestion, running models on-premise instead of renting someone else's cloud, and the validation layers that decide whether a system may act, without giving up security, compliance, or the ability to explain a decision afterwards.

Audience
Professional engineers, technical directors, project managers, operations heads
Format
In-person technical session. 45 minute presentation followed by 15 minutes of questions
Level
Technical, but no machine learning background assumed
Status
Scheduled with the Institution of Engineers, Malaysia

Previously

AI in everyday life: from chatting to autopilot

A general-audience talk on how one person runs a technology company with AI, and what that shift means for an established business.

It opens by asking the room to tell real photographs from generated ones. Nobody can, including the person who builds these systems for a living: the tools have crossed a line most people have not had time to notice.

From there: the shift from chatting with AI to letting it do the work, what one person with agents can now run, which parts of a business move first, and what stays firmly human. The room votes with raised hands throughout.

Audience
Business owners, professionals and a general audience; no technical background assumed
Format
In-person talk with live audience participation
Level
General. If you have only ever heard of ChatGPT, that is enough
Status
