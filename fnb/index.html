The problem

Where the margin goes.

Ordering runs on gut feel

The order goes in days ahead on last week's memory, so a wet Tuesday leaves the chiller full and a holiday empties it by noon.

The bin keeps no ledger

What went into it this week had a price, but nothing was named or weighed on the way, so the loss vanishes into the food cost line.

Margins age between stocktakes

Ingredient prices move under the menu weekly, and which dishes still earn their place is discovered a month later, at the stocktake.

Reviews wait for a quiet hour

They arrive in three languages while service is at full stretch, the quiet hour never comes, and the silence is what the next diner reads.
