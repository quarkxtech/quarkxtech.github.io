Chiller plant optimisation

The plant runs itself.

It reads your existing building automation historian and executes supervised control actions back into the plant. Continuously, on-premise, around the clock.

Software only. No equipment replacement On-premise. Data never leaves your building Live at a Malaysian shopping mall

The problem

Your biggest electrical load is tuned by hand, once.

In most malls, hotels, hospitals and data centres, the chiller plant is the building's single largest electrical load. Very few run anywhere near their best operating point.

Too many variables, too few operators

Staging, setpoints, pump speed, fan logic: the best combination changes with load and weather several times an hour. No team can retune that continuously.

Automation schedules are frozen at commissioning

Automation systems run sequences written years ago. They do not adapt to load drift, ageing equipment or seasonal change.

The data is already there, unread

Plants log thousands of points a minute into a historian. The optimisation signal is in that database; the control system never sees it.

Retrofit cannot be justified

Replacing chillers is a capital project with months of disruption. For most owners the payback never clears the bar.

What it does

It closes the loop.

Most analytics products stop at a dashboard recommendation and leave the acting to a busy person. This system completes the circuit.

How it is built

A small server in your plant room.

No cloud, no third-party platform, and no operational data leaving your perimeter.

Edge server
Runs inside your plant room. No cloud, no external platform in the control path.
Private link
Encrypted access under named, auditable accounts. Plant data is never publicly exposed.
Data engine
Reads high-frequency time-series data from the historian you already run.
Optimisation
Machine learning with physics-grounded models: chiller sequencing, condenser setpoints, pumps, storage, cooling-tower fans.
Independent check
Every decision is evaluated separately before it may act. Nothing reviews its own output.
Control layer
Executes approved actions back into the automation system, within documented intervention bands.

Design principles

Four commitments we will put in the contract.

Data sovereignty by default

Operational data stays on your infrastructure. Fully on-premise, with engineer access over an encrypted link under named accounts.

Mechanically validated control

Every action is checked against the working rules of our partner mechanical practice, built on more than 25 years of hands-on chiller work. Nothing ships without a sound mechanical reason.

Supervised and reversible

Every action is logged and stays inside documented intervention bands. Your team can pause or reverse any of them at any time.

Measurement before action

Instrumentation goes in before anything changes. The baseline is agreed in writing and verified by an independent reviewer at settlement.

Commercials

If we save nothing, you pay nothing.

No capital outlay. You are only ever invoiced against savings an independent reviewer has verified.
