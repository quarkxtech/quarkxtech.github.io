Chiller plant optimisation

The plant runs itself.

The system reads your existing building automation historian, reasons over the data, and executes supervised control actions back into the plant. Continuously, on-premise, around the clock.

Software only. No equipment replacement On-premise. Data never leaves your building Live at a Malaysian shopping mall

The problem

Your biggest electrical load is tuned by hand, once.

In most malls, hotels, hospitals, offices and data centres, the chiller plant is the single largest consumer of electricity in the building. Very few of them are running anywhere near their best operating point.

Too many variables, too few operators

Chiller staging, condenser water setpoint, pump speed, cooling-tower fan logic. The best combination changes with load and weather, several times an hour. No team can retune that continuously.

Automation schedules are frozen at commissioning

Building automation systems run sequences written years ago. They cannot learn from the historian, and they do not adapt to load drift, ageing equipment or seasonal change.

The data is already there, unread

Plants log thousands of points per minute into a historian. The optimisation signal is sitting in that database. The control system never sees it.

Retrofit cannot be justified

Replacing chillers or upgrading the automation platform is a capital project with months of disruption. For most owners the payback never clears the bar.

What it does

It closes the loop.

Most building analytics products stop at a recommendation on a dashboard, and leave the acting to a person who is busy. This system completes the circuit.

How it is built

A small server in your plant room.

No cloud, no third-party platform, and no operational data leaving your perimeter.

Edge server
Runs inside your plant room. No cloud dependency and no external platform in the control path.
Private link
Encrypted engineer access under named, auditable accounts. Plant data is never publicly exposed.
Data engine
Reads high-frequency time-series data from the historian you already run.
Optimisation
Machine learning combined with physics-grounded models, covering chiller sequencing, condenser water setpoint, pump control, storage charge and discharge, and cooling-tower fan logic.
Independent check
Every decision is evaluated separately before it is permitted to act. Nothing reviews its own output.
Control layer
Executes approved actions back into the automation system, within documented intervention bands.

Design principles

Four commitments we will put in the contract.

Data sovereignty by default

Operational data stays on your infrastructure. the system runs fully on-premise, and engineer access is via encrypted link under named accounts.

Mechanically validated control

Every control action is checked against the working rules of the mechanical engineering practice we partner with, built on more than 25 years of hands-on chiller plant work. Nothing ships without a sound mechanical reason.

Supervised and reversible

The loop runs under named supervision with documented intervention bands. Every action is logged, and your team can pause or reverse any of them at any time.

Measurement before action

Instrumentation goes in before anything is changed. The baseline is agreed with you in writing and benchmarked by an independent reviewer at settlement.

Commercials

If we save nothing, you pay nothing.

It is deployed under a performance-based agreement. There is no capital outlay, and you are only ever invoiced against savings that an independent reviewer has verified.
