How we work

We do not build what nobody asked for.

A system gets built when an operator has the problem and the contract is signed. That is the opposite of how most AI is sold, and it is why what we ship fits the way your operation actually runs.

On-premise by default

Your operational data stays inside your infrastructure. This is a design principle, not a paid upgrade.

Measured before it is claimed

Baselines are agreed with you in writing before anything changes, and benchmarked by an independent reviewer at settlement. We would rather be checked than believed.

Supervised and reversible

Every action is logged and runs within documented intervention bands. Your team can pause or reverse anything, at any time, without calling us first.

Nothing reviews its own work

Build and review are separated by standing policy. The reviewer receives the artefact and the standard, never the reasoning that produced it.

Good questions

Asked by operators. Answered by engineers.

What does QuarkX actually do?

We build software that closes the loop. Traditional AI stops at a dashboard recommendation and waits for a busy person to act. Our systems observe the environment, reason about its state, and carry out the action themselves, within limits your team has agreed in writing and can pause or reverse at any time.

What is an "agentic AI" system?

A normal AI tool answers when asked. An agentic system is given a job and the tools to do it: it watches its environment, decides what to do next, does it, and checks the result. It is the difference between a weather forecast and a system that adjusts the climate itself.

Can you share who your clients are?

Not by name: we default to strict NDAs, and our clients' operating data is commercially sensitive. What we can say is that our systems run live in commercial property in Malaysia, with a trading-operations deployment being scoped, alongside AI engineering for a European technology company. If you have an operational bottleneck, we are happy to discuss what we have built, confidentially.

How do you handle data security and privacy?

Sovereignty is a core design principle. Unlike standard cloud-first AI, our agentic systems are architected to run on-premise or within your private cloud. This ensures your mission-critical operational data never leaves your controlled infrastructure, providing absolute privacy and eliminating reliance on external cloud services.

What makes QuarkX different?

The rare overlap of disciplines: Cambridge computational modelling, low-level systems engineering on global telecommunications infrastructure, and production-scale agent orchestration at a world-leading fintech platform. In practice it means the same person who understands the physics also writes the code that touches your plant.

How do we begin?

Drop us an email. We start with an exploratory technical discussion to drill into your operational challenge. No commitment, no sales pitch, just a frank conversation about whether agentic architecture is the right solution.

What happens if you are unavailable?

Your operation does not depend on us being reachable. Systems run on your infrastructure rather than ours. Extracted and computed data is written to open formats as part of normal running, so your records already sit in files you control. Control actions stay inside documented intervention bands and can be paused or reversed by your own team without calling us. Documentation and deployment guides are delivered with the work, not promised after it. And on the mechanical side we are not alone: every control action in the plant system is checked against the working rules of an engineering practice with more than 25 years of chiller experience.

Who owns what you build for us?

Ownership and licensing are set out in writing in the engagement agreement before work starts, rather than left to be discovered at the point you need to know. Whatever the arrangement, two things do not vary: your operational data is yours, and it is exported to open formats you can read without our software.
