Asked by operators. Answered by engineers.

What does QuarkX actually do?

Traditional AI stops at a dashboard recommendation and waits for a busy person to act. Our systems close the loop: they observe, reason, and act themselves, within written limits your team can pause or reverse at any time.

What is an "agentic AI" system?

A normal AI tool answers when asked. An agentic system is given a job and does it: watching, deciding, acting, checking. The difference between a weather forecast and a system that adjusts the climate itself.

Can you share who your clients are?

Not by name: we default to strict NDAs. What we can say: our systems run live in commercial property in Malaysia, alongside systems engineering for a European technology company. The rest we discuss confidentially.

How do you handle data security and privacy?

Our systems run on-premise or inside your private cloud, never on someone else's servers. Operational data does not leave your controlled infrastructure.

What makes QuarkX different?

A rare overlap: Cambridge computational modelling, low-level engineering on global telecommunications infrastructure, and production-scale agent orchestration at a world-leading fintech platform. The person who understands the physics also writes the code that touches your operation.

How do we begin?

Drop us an email. We start with a frank technical conversation about whether this kind of system fits your problem: no commitment, no sales pitch.

What happens if you are unavailable?

Systems run on your infrastructure, not ours, and your records are written to open formats as part of normal running. Your team can pause or reverse any action without calling us, and documentation is delivered with the work, not promised after it. Where a system touches physical equipment, every control action is checked against the working rules of an engineering practice with more than 25 years of plant experience.

Who owns what you build for us?

Nothing is built without a signed brief: we design against your brief, and the brief stays yours. Ownership and licensing are set out in writing before work starts. Two things never vary: your operational data is yours, and it is exported to open formats you can read without our software.
